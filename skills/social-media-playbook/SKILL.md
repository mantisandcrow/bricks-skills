# Social Media Service Offering — Mantis & Crow

## Service Scope

### Social Media Setup (Once-Off)

**Facebook & Instagram Business Setup**
- Business page creation
- Profile optimization
- Branded cover and profile images
- Content strategy consultation
- Initial post scheduling

**LinkedIn Business Page Setup**
- Company page creation and configuration
- Branded banner and logo
- Employee profile guidance
- Initial post templates

**Google My Business**
- Business listing creation
- NAP consistency (Name, Address, Phone)
- Category selection
- Opening hours configuration
- Photos and initial review strategy

### Social Media Management (Monthly Retainer)

- 12 branded posts/month cross-posted across Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn
- 1 LinkedIn article/month (thought leadership or product-focused)
- Content calendar and scheduling
- Performance reporting

## Pricing (ZAR)

- Setup: R6,000 once-off
- Monthly retainer: R6,000/month

## Proposal Structure

Phase 3 covers Social Media Setup:
- Facebook & Instagram Business Setup
- LinkedIn Business Page Setup
- Google My Business Setup
- Initial content strategy

Phase 4 covers Ongoing Management:
- Monthly content calendar
- Cross-platform posting
- Performance reporting

## Content Guidelines

- Use brand colors (#0D141C, #1EB473, #FAFBFC)
- Maintain consistent voice and tone
- Include calls to action
- Tag relevant accounts
- Use appropriate hashtags per platform
- Rotate content types (image, video, carousel, text)

## Platform-Specific Notes

**Facebook/Instagram**
- Business accounts required for ads
- Connect to Meta Business Suite
- Set up Facebook Pixel for tracking

**LinkedIn**
- Company page needed for employee advocacy
- Article publishing for thought leadership
- Professional tone required

**Google My Business**
- Keep hours updated
- Respond to reviews
- Post weekly updates
- Add photos regularly
